## 1. Purpose and governing rule

This catalogue is a source-indexed consolidation of DingoOS principles recovered from accessible Side-bet branches, the DingoOS public Project Board, relevant Library records and the active user conversation.

It is not a replacement for the original Engineering Foundations Principles, the Universal Engineering Methods Manual, the canonical architecture, a project charter, an Owner Decision register or an existing operational authority.

The governing rule is:

**Recover what existed, establish what is true, derive what follows, and only then claim what is new.**

The catalogue must preserve the distinction between:
1. authenticated original principles and manuals;
2. adopted programme directives and binding requirements;
3. user-directed principles recorded in an identified source;
4. principles explicitly agreed in the active conversation but not yet incorporated into a canonical manual;
5. controlled proposals awaiting review or adoption;
6. historical progress records and derived interpretations;
7. unresolved source material.

A principle's appearance in this catalogue does not, by itself, make it canonically adopted.

### Family A — Human agency, authorship and intellectual freedom

**A-01 — Human narrative sovereignty**  
The user/originator retains the right to direct, influence, challenge, interrupt, revise and redirect the intellectual and developmental narrative. AI recommendations are not user decisions.  
**Status:** USER-AGREED — CONVERSATION, 28 November 2026.

**A-02 — Human decision authority**  
Consequential acceptance, adoption, release, disclosure and authorization remain with the appropriately authorized human decision-maker. Technical capability or AI confidence does not create permission.  
**Status:** Adopted programme boundary; also reflected in the public α–β–γ–Σ interface contract and UNESCO human-oversight reference.

**A-03 — Preserve authorial intent**  
Record the user's stated objective, constraints, decisions and intended scope. Distinguish them from assistant interpretations, inferred preferences and recommendations. Do not silently rewrite an instruction or substitute a new objective.  
**Status:** USER-AGREED direction and Side-bet continuation mandate.

**A-04 — Freedom to explore; discipline in conclusions**  
Unconventional, unfamiliar and competing ideas may be investigated without prejudicial dismissal. Their acceptance still depends on the appropriate mathematical, scientific, engineering, ethical and evidential requirements.  
**Status:** Derived normalization of user-directed intellectual freedom, concept-expansion records and belief-neutral evaluation.

**A-05 — No forced consensus**  
Preserve disagreement, dissent, unresolved objections and alternative explanations. Consensus, confidence, reputation or majority preference is not a substitute for evidence.  
**Status:** Historical public-board policy and controlled platform proposal.

### Family B — Recovery, continuity and source integrity

**B-01 — Recover before developing**  
Recover relevant original documents, prior decisions, equations, designs, code, test records and revision histories before extending or replacing them.  
**Status:** ADOPTED — PROGRAMME DIRECTIVE.

**B-02 — Continue existing work by default**  
When the user identifies prior work or asks to resume, enter continuation/recovery mode rather than greenfield design, unless the user explicitly authorizes a restart.  
**Status:** Side-bet mandate; AI Research Recovery Assurance Rev0.2 defines continuation and scope controls as a proposed integration profile.

**B-03 — Multi-surface source recovery**  
Search the relevant conversations, attachments, complete accessible Library, root-level and nested files, source-bearing compilations, archives, repositories, branches, commits, pull requests, issues and connected sources. Record each surface and its coverage state.  
**Status:** CONTROLLED PROPOSAL in EMM-ADD-001 Rev0.2; RE-001 is ADOPTED — BINDING.

**B-04 — Scoped absence claims only**  
A failed lookup, 404, empty folder, search snippet or inaccessible repository cannot establish global absence. State exactly what was searched and what remains unsearched, inaccessible or uninspected.  
**Status:** ADOPTED through RE-001 and the Side-bet mandate.

**B-05 — Preserve originals**  
Preserve authenticated original artifacts byte-for-byte. Corrections, annotations, extracts and reconstructions must be new, linked artifacts; never silently overwrite the only original.  
**Status:** ADOPTED — PROGRAMME DIRECTIVE and RE-003 lineage requirement.

**B-06 — Source identity and revision integrity**  
Record exact source identity, owner, repository/path, branch, commit, revision, timestamps, retrieval method and hashes where available. Distinguish current originals, public/sanitized mirrors, historical compilations, generated material and derived work.  
**Status:** ADOPTED in principle through the Side-bet mandate and RE-002/RE-003.

**B-07 — No substitution or retroactive attribution**  
Do not replace missing originals with plausible reconstructions; do not attribute new derivations to the original author or foundation without provenance; do not promote a benchmark into proof of an unretrieved theory.  
**Status:** ADOPTED — PROGRAMME DIRECTIVE and RE-003.

**B-08 — Keep lineages separate**  
Compare historical versions pairwise and preserve conflicts. A synthesis or reconstruction must be a new derived node linked to all source nodes and transformations. Never silently merge distinct revisions into a fictitious canonical artifact.  
**Status:** ADOPTED — BINDING through RE-003.

**B-09 — Recover dependencies, not merely filenames**  
For consequential operations, trace definitions, callers, dispatch paths, authorization, validation, persistence, effecting targets, receipts, retries, recovery paths and tests. A name or declaration alone does not establish actual operation or enforcement.  
**Status:** ADOPTED — BINDING through RE-004.

**B-10 — Continuity and correction propagation**  
Recover prior parameters, revisions, decisions and unresolved work; identify contradictions; propagate corrections through dependent claims and artifacts while preserving historical records.  
**Status:** Side-bet mandate and RE-005; detailed operational integration remains subject to existing NCP-03 semantics.

### Family C — Truth, evidence and epistemic integrity

**C-01 — Belief-neutral evaluation**  
A proposition is not accepted or rejected because someone believes it, disbelieves it, prefers it or holds status. Evaluate it by the evidence and standards appropriate to its proposition type.  
**Status:** HISTORICAL PROGRESS RECORD — DingoOS Belief-Neutral Evidence and Mathematical Validity Policy, 10 October 2026.

**C-02 — Match the evidence standard to the claim**  
Mathematical claims require explicit premises, valid inference and proof or counterexample; empirical claims require appropriate observations, controls, uncertainty and replication where feasible; historical claims require identifiable sources, provenance and context; engineering decisions require requirements, tests, hazards, operating limits and authorization.  
**Status:** Historical policy record; consistent with adopted recovery and evidence requirements.

**C-03 — Separate proof, empirical support, maturity and authorization**  
Formal proof, empirical evidence, source authenticity, model maturity, operational status, verification, validation, qualification and human authorization are different state dimensions. One cannot be used as a proxy for another.  
**Status:** Historical theorem-registry and research-evaluation records; Side-bet claim discipline.

**C-04 — Objective reality and observation separation**  
Distinguish the physical system, an observation, a measurement, an interpretation, a mathematical model, a simulation, a prediction, qualified evidence and an authorized action. “Objective” means scoped, transparent, reproducible and open to challenge—not infallible.  
**Status:** Historical Objective Reality Environment progress record.

**C-05 — Claim-to-evidence traceability**  
Every material claim must identify the source, location, revision, method, assumptions, limitations, uncertainty, contradictory evidence and review state that support it. A citation is insufficient if the source does not actually support the claim.  
**Status:** ADOPTED programme direction; RE-001–RE-003 and AI Research Recovery Assurance Rev0.2 controls.

**C-06 — Falsifiability and alternative explanations**  
Material hypotheses and engineering claims must identify possible failure conditions, discriminating tests and relevant alternative explanations. Preserve null, failed and contradictory results.  
**Status:** Historical research-evaluation and concept-expansion records; objective-innovation direction.

**C-07 — Uncertainty and limitations are first-class**  
Record measurement uncertainty, model validity domains, data-quality limits, numerical error, source uncertainty and incomplete coverage. Do not hide uncertainty through false precision or confidence language.  
**Status:** Historical research/evidence contracts and controlled PIDD/IVET proposals.

**C-08 — No evidence laundering through summaries**  
Memory, summaries, search snippets, generated text and projections are discovery aids, not primary-source authentication. Retrieve and inspect the underlying artifact before claiming it is recovered.  
**Status:** Side-bet recovery mandate and AI Research Recovery Assurance Rev0.2 incident controls.

### Family D — Mathematical and scientific foundations

**D-01 — Mathematical integrity**  
Every equation or theorem must have identifiable definitions, domains, assumptions, notation, dimensions, units and limitations. Check algebraic, logical and dimensional validity.

**D-02 — Derive from foundations**  
Derivations must be traceable to explicit premises and valid transformations. Do not use the desired conclusion as its own justification.

**D-03 — Reverse-first and forward reconstruction**  
Recover the mathematical origin, reconstruct the forward development, then trace the destination backwards through every dependency. Reconcile the two paths; investigate discrepancies rather than forcing agreement.  
**Status:** USER-AGREED — CONVERSATION, 28 November 2026. Canonical manual integration pending.

**D-04 — Complete the chain, not only the endpoint**  
Identify missing definitions, intermediate derivations, assumptions and physical links. Solve a missing link when a valid solution can be established; otherwise state the precise obstruction or unresolved requirement.

**D-05 — Independent reproduction**  
Important derivations and calculations should be checked by a distinct method, implementation, solver, dataset or reviewer where practicable. Repeating the same method with a second agent is not necessarily independent verification.

**D-06 — Distinguish computation from proof and experiment**  
Symbolic computation, numerical simulation, software tests, analytical proof, prototype demonstration, calibrated measurement and physical validation are different forms of evidence. State exactly what each establishes.

**D-07 — Units, metrology and sensitivity**  
Use explicit units and conventions; track instrument identity and calibration for measurements; quantify uncertainty; test sensitivity to assumptions and parameters.

**Status for Family D:** Derived consolidation of the user-approved Reverse-First direction and historical mathematics, theorem, objective-reality and research-evaluation records. The original complete Engineering Foundations Principles text and canonical mathematics manual remain UNRETRIEVED / not authenticated in the current source pass.

### Family E — Objective innovation and competitive development

**E-01 — Objective Innovation**  
The user defines objective innovation as:

> The systematic pursuit of technological advancement guided by empirical evidence, mathematical rigor, and measurable performance criteria rather than intuition, hype, or subjective preference.

**Status:** USER-DIRECTED PRINCIPLE, recorded in Side-bet Issue #4.

**E-02 — Evidence-driven selection**  
Compare alternatives using fit-for-purpose quantitative measures, explicit baselines, uncertainty, cost/performance, scalability, resource and lifecycle requirements, and declared constraints. Mandatory safety, rights and correctness constraints cannot be offset by a weighted score.  
**Status:** User-directed Objective Innovation record; detailed method remains to be reconciled with canonical CDE/PIDD.

**E-03 — Falsifiability in engineering**  
Design prototype tests to expose failure modes early. Specify controls, calibration, repeatability, raw data, stopping rules and explicit failure criteria.  
**Status:** User-directed Objective Innovation record.

**E-04 — Traceability to physical reality**  
Connect technology claims and performance targets to relevant physical principles, equations, model assumptions, valid domains, measurement methods, calibration and uncertainty.  
**Status:** User-directed Objective Innovation record.

**E-05 — Explore materially distinct alternatives before consequential commitment**  
Avoid premature design fixation. For substantial decisions, use a reference baseline plus at least two materially distinct candidate methods where a meaningful baseline exists. Candidate diversity concerns underlying principle, architecture, algorithm, material, manufacturing route, energy/information path or function allocation—not cosmetic parameter changes.  
**Status:** CONTROLLED PROPOSAL in EMM-ADD-001 Rev0.2; related prior CDE V1 work is referenced but current canonical integration is not reauthenticated.

**E-06 — Freeze common requirements and gates**  
Freeze the objective, requirements, constraints, safety/rights gates, test protocol and resource-accounting basis before comparing candidates. If requirements change, re-baseline all candidates or begin a new comparison round.

**E-07 — Fair and comparable candidate development**  
Develop candidates to comparable fidelity and effort. Disclose shared models, prompts, code, data or reviewers that limit independence. Avoid strawman comparisons.

**E-08 — Multi-objective and Pareto comparison**  
Expose trade-offs across performance, safety, cost, reliability, maintainability, lifecycle, integration, resources and uncertainty. Do not conceal unresolved trade-offs in one arbitrary score. Mandatory gates remain pass/fail.

**E-09 — Preserve rejected candidates and failures**  
Retain designs, assumptions, evidence, failure conditions, dissent and reasons for rejection. A rejected approach may become useful when technology or knowledge changes.

**E-10 — Progressive fidelity and value of information**  
Use progressively more costly analysis and tests as candidates mature. Choose discriminating experiments that could change the decision. Do not develop every concept to production depth without evidence that the additional effort is valuable.

**E-11 — Select, combine, defer or retain**  
Possible outcomes include select, combine compatible strengths into a new separately identified candidate, retain multiple approaches, defer for evidence, reject with reasons or reopen candidate generation. No candidate must be selected simply because a decision deadline has arrived.

**Status for Family E:** Objective Innovation is user-directed. EMM-ADD-001 Rev0.2 defines PIDD-0 through PIDD-10 as a controlled proposed addendum, not yet incorporated into an authenticated canonical manual.

### Family F — Whole-system engineering and architecture

**F-01 — Whole-System Thinking**  
Assess local components in the context of the complete system, environment, dependencies, interfaces, safety and lifecycle.

**F-02 — Integration by Design**  
Consider integration, interface contracts, data, timing, resources, authority and verification from the beginning rather than as a final assembly task.

**F-03 — Adjacency, parallelism and dependency integrity**  
Distinguish genuine sequential dependencies from components that can develop independently. Parallel scheduling does not prove technical independence; shared foundations and coupling must be visible.

**F-04 — Multiple development pathways**  
Retain viable alternatives while uncertainty remains. Do not prematurely force all work into one design or architecture.

**F-05 — Evolutionary and extensible design**  
Support controlled extension, compatibility, versioning and future technology development without destabilizing established foundations unnecessarily.

**F-06 — One normative home; reuse existing authorities**  
Do not create duplicate registries, ledgers, workflows, event stores, schemas, approval authorities or test families where canonical owners already exist. Put normative rules in one authenticated home and cross-reference them elsewhere.

**F-07 — Interface and ownership clarity**  
For every subsystem or interface, establish the responsible owner, inputs, outputs, dependencies, version, validation, security, persistence and failure semantics from source evidence. Do not assume a proposed architecture or acronym is canonical.

**Status for Family F:** Whole-system integration, recursion and reuse are documented in public-board progress records and current DingoOS proposals. The original canonical architecture and Universal Engineering Methods Manual have not been fully reauthenticated in this pass.

### Family G — Research, laboratories and physical validation

**G-01 — Preserve discipline boundaries while enabling justified integration**  
Physics Lab and Technology Lab are separate workstreams with complementary roles. Working distinction pending charter recovery:
- Physics Lab: physical principles, mathematical model claims, physical measurement, uncertainty and physical validation.
- Technology Lab: research, design, compare, prototype and qualify advanced technologies against requirements using established or explicitly testable principles.

Shared mathematics and evidence infrastructure should be reused through existing canonical owners; neither lab should create duplicate registries or qualification authorities.

**G-02 — Experimental protocol integrity**  
Define the hypothesis, protocol, controls, raw-data requirements, instrument identity, calibration, uncertainty, repeatability and failure criteria before relying on an experiment.

**G-03 — Physical evidence chain**  
Connect observations to instruments, calibration, methods, timing, raw-data custody, transformations, uncertainty and model predictions.

**G-04 — Preserve failed and null experiments**  
Maintain failed convergence, negative measurements, failed prototypes and contradictory outcomes as durable evidence with context. Do not silently replace or delete them.

**G-05 — No simulation-to-reality promotion**  
A simulated result, numerically tested benchmark or software test cannot be presented as an experimental measurement or physical validation. Each advancement in evidence maturity requires its own supporting evidence.

**G-06 — Domain-specific validation**  
A mathematical model or physical benchmark is valid only within its declared assumptions, boundary conditions and tested domain. Do not infer a broad physical theory from unrelated benchmark fit or limited test success.

**Status for Family G:** User-directed workstream distinction and lab recovery records. The canonical Physics Lab and Technology Lab charters and complete inventories remain open recovery items. SW-ZPE-01, UAP mathematics, Nano-Glass and other programmes retain separate project-specific requirements.

### Family H — Human/AI collaboration and independent assurance

**H-01 — Assist the creator; do not become the creator**  
AI is an investigative and engineering assistant. It must not silently become the author of the user's intentions, original concepts or decisions.  
**Status:** USER-AGREED — CONVERSATION, 28 November 2026.

**H-02 — Separate producer, coordinator, verifier and authority**  
A producer does not self-certify. A coordinator cannot turn its own synthesis into verification. A verifier cannot grant authorization outside its delegated role. The canonical C2PO–C5PO meanings and responsibility boundaries must be recovered before treating proposed role mappings as authoritative.

**H-03 — Independence must be demonstrated**  
An independent check must use a meaningfully distinct method, data source, implementation, instrument or reviewer where required. Shared models, prompts, retrieval corpora and code paths can create correlated errors and must be disclosed.

**H-04 — Bounded autonomy**  
AI agents and tools operate within declared scope and permissions. Consequential external effects require the applicable authorization at the effecting boundary, with current revocation and recovery semantics.

**H-05 — Transparency, uncertainty and abstention**  
AI must disclose when it has not inspected a source, lacks required evidence, is inferring, is proposing or cannot verify a result. Prefer a scoped partial answer or abstention over an unsupported completion claim.

**H-06 — Consent, privacy and non-coercion**  
Concept-expansion and human-inquiry work must preserve consent, privacy, retention limits, non-leading capture and human authorization. Subjective report, interpretation, hypothesis and qualified evidence remain separate.

**Status for Family H:** User-agreed human agency addition; historical concept-expansion and hybrid-humanity records; controlled AI recovery and platform proposals. Runtime enforcement is not established by these documents alone.

### Family I — Rights, security and consequential governance

**I-01 — Protect original and background IP**  
Preserve protected DingoOS/BIF originals and private material. Public Side-bet branches contain only material authorized for public release. A public-safe index may point to a private source without reproducing it.

**I-02 — Authorization is distinct from capability**  
A tool's ability to act, a valid signature, a local status or a UI control is not, by itself, authorization for a consequential effect.

**I-03 — Enforce at the effecting boundary**  
For consequential actions, establish that the actual target/resource enforces the required authority, scope, revocation and fencing semantics at commitment. A coordinator-side check or local ledger append alone does not prove target enforcement.

**I-04 — Fail closed when authority or evidence is uncertain**  
Unknown, stale, revoked, conflicting or unverified authorization/effect state must not be promoted to success. Preserve HOLD / NO-GO where the governing control requires it.

**I-05 — Least privilege, confidentiality and disclosure control**  
Protect credentials, secrets, private user data and confidential source material. Apply access controls at retrieval, indexing, model-context, export and release boundaries.

**Status for Family I:** Side-bet workspace mandate and adopted RE requirements establish relevant boundaries. Detailed IP and execution control implementation must be checked against their canonical owners; consequential dispatch remains HOLD / NO-GO until target-side enforcement and evidence are established.

### Family J — Verification, recovery and lifecycle improvement

**J-01 — Verify before acceptance**  
Acceptance must be tied to the exact artifact revision, configuration, environment, criteria and evidence. Authored tests, passing fixture tests, integration verification, physical validation and release authorization must remain distinct.

**J-02 — Reproducibility**  
Record inputs, parameters, versions, tools, environment, random seeds where applicable, commands, raw logs, exit codes, outputs and hashes so an independent reviewer can repeat the result.

**J-03 — Risk-proportionate assurance**  
Scale independence, test depth and review effort to consequence, uncertainty, reversibility and exposure. Do not weaken hard safety or rights constraints to satisfy schedule or score.

**J-04 — Audit-to-repair, not audit-only**  
A substantive audit should identify actionable remediation opportunities, research them, rank them, distinguish authorized changes from blocked proposals, verify any implemented correction and create recurrence controls. This process does not authorize changes to protected IP or production systems.  
**Status:** CONTROLLED PROPOSAL in DINGOOS-AI-RECOVERY-001 Rev0.1; not canonically adopted.

**J-05 — Recursive correction and reassessment**  
New evidence may reopen an earlier result. Identify affected dependencies, invalidate or supersede the affected claims through the existing authority and retest where required. Do not silently rewrite history.

**J-06 — Preserve history and append corrections**  
Retain prior records, failed results, decision rationale and supersession links. Retention does not imply truth; it preserves the ability to audit, challenge and correct.

**J-07 — Quality over speed**  
Correctness, safety, provenance, reproducibility and integration quality take priority over superficial output volume or premature closure.

**J-08 — Measure improvement**  
Measure real outcomes—defect escape, recovery performance, uncertainty reduction, verified reuse, candidate diversity, user outcomes, resource consumption and reproducibility. Do not claim exponential or compounding improvement without longitudinal evidence.

**J-09 — Free-first and resource-aware development**  
Prefer local, free, replaceable tooling where technically appropriate. Do not introduce paid services, billing dependencies or external APIs as assumed requirements without explicit approval.

**J-10 — Responsible lifecycle and sustainability**  
Consider accessibility, human review burden, safety, resource/energy use, maintainability, lifecycle costs and environmental consequences where relevant to the system.

**Status for Family J:** Mixed: established historical programme directions, Side-bet-adopted requirements and controlled proposals. The canonical manual and implementation status must be reconciled before declaring every entry binding.

## 6. Domain and project application boundaries

Universal principles govern the way DingoOS works. They do not erase the distinct purpose or evidence requirements of individual domains.

- **BIF:** preserve original intellectual property and source lineage. This public catalogue contains no reconstructed BIF equations or protected implementation detail.
- **Physics Lab:** physical principles, mathematical model claims, measurement, uncertainty and physical validation. The actual charter, index and complete physical evidence corpus remain open recovery items.
- **Technology Lab:** advanced technology research, design, comparison, prototyping and qualification. Its canonical charter and complete capability inventory remain open recovery items.
- **Mathematics/theorem foundations:** retain explicit definitions, assumptions, proof state, empirical state, source lineage and authorization as separate dimensions.
- **SW-ZPE-01:** a separate applied engineering workstream with its own design basis, parameter and measurement controls. Do not merge its project-specific principles into the universal catalogue except where a genuinely universal rule applies.
- **UAP mathematics investigation:** separate research workstream; maintain strict separation between measured evidence, derived results, assumptions and speculation.
- **Nano-Glass Holographic Technology:** a separate future-technology research programme; preserve multiple independently investigable technology branches and evidence-led evolution without prematurely fixing speculative dimensions or materials.
- **Other programmes:** inherit the universal rules and add their own domain-specific constraints, acceptance gates and evidence requirements.

Shared mathematical, provenance, knowledge, verification and workflow capabilities must be reused through recovered canonical interfaces rather than recreated as parallel registries.

## 7. External standards and research alignment

The following sources provide external reference points. They do not establish DingoOS authorship, adoption, conformance or implementation.

1. **ISO/IEC/IEEE 15288:2023**, systems and software engineering life-cycle processes. It provides a common life-cycle process framework applicable across systems and system-of-systems, with processes usable iteratively, concurrently and recursively. It does not prescribe a particular development method or architecture.
   - https://www.iso.org/standard/81702.html
2. **NASA Systems Engineering Handbook**, system design and verification/validation. Its design process describes iterative validation of alternative design concepts against stakeholder expectations and asks whether systems work as expected and respond to failures.
   - https://science.nasa.gov/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/nasa_systems_engineering_handbook_0.pdf
3. **W3C PROV-O**, provenance ontology. Its Entity, Activity and Agent concepts and derivation/attribution relationships provide a useful external vocabulary for provenance modelling.
   - https://www.w3.org/TR/prov-o/
